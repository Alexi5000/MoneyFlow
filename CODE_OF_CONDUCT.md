# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

MoneyFlow welcomes contributors, reviewers, and users from diverse backgrounds. Participate with respect, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and protect the privacy of others. Discussion should focus on observable behavior, evidence, and maintainable engineering decisions.

## Expected behavior

Use inclusive language; welcome good-faith questions; provide specific, constructive review; acknowledge uncertainty; and keep real financial, personal, and credential data out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sharing private financial information or credentials, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Do not use public channels to disclose vulnerabilities.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or limit participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
